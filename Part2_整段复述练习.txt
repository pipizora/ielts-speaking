对结果开心的重要决定
故事线：国贸不感兴趣 → 考试转英语 → 补课 → 更合适

句首提示：
第1段：An important decision I was happy … → I made the … → The original course didn't interest …
第2段：The decision itself wasn't especially … → I took the exams … → In my second year, I also …
第3段：English wasn't some lifelong dream, … → It was much broader …
第4段：Looking back, I'm … → The subject suited me better, … → There was extra work involved, but …

参考回答：
An important decision I was happy with was changing my university major from international trade to English. I made the decision after my first year. The original course didn't interest me, and I particularly disliked the maths involved.
The decision itself wasn't especially difficult because I knew I didn't want to continue. I took the exams required by the foreign languages department and transferred. In my second year, I also had to catch up on the first-year courses I'd missed.
English wasn't some lifelong dream, but the course introduced me to literature, interpreting and debate. It was much broader than the English I'd learned at school.
Looking back, I'm glad I changed direction. The subject suited me better, and my language background later became useful in overseas marketing. There was extra work involved, but continuing with a subject I disliked would have been harder to justify.
